Risks / ความเสี่ยง

  • Extreme valuation: ~68x sales and ~150x trailing earnings leaves no room for disappointment — even a good quarter that merely meets expectations can trigger a sharp drawdown.
  • AI-spending cycle risk: much of the growth is tied to the current corporate AI investment boom; if AI budgets cool or the 'AI bubble' deflates, commercial bookings could slow quickly.
  • Government/customer concentration: US government work is roughly 40%+ of revenue, exposed to budget fights, election-driven priority shifts, and contract timing.
  • Competition: hyperscalers (Microsoft, Google, AWS), Snowflake/Databricks, and defense-tech startups are all attacking the same AI-platform and government-software budgets.
  • Regulation and reputation: surveillance, defense, and immigration-related work draws political and ESG scrutiny in the US and Europe, which can cost contracts and multiple.

What would break the thesis / อะไรจะทำให้เหตุผลนี้พัง

  • Q3 2026 guidance or results implying revenue growth decelerating sharply (e.g. Q3 revenue below the guided $2.16B, or FY guide cut) — the ~80-90% growth story is what supports the multiple.
  • US commercial revenue growth falling from triple digits toward ~50% or below — this segment is the core of the AI momentum narrative.
  • Broad AI-capex sentiment rolling over (major cloud/AI players cutting spend), which would compress high-multiple AI software names regardless of Palantir's own execution.
  • Loss or material shrinkage of a flagship US government contract, or a US budget impasse delaying government revenue recognition.
